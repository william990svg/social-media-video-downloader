    Copy the link

    2

    Common questions

    Is this downloader free?

    Yes — fetching links and saving videos costs nothing, and there is no account to create. Use it as many times as you like.

    Which link should I paste?

    The share link of a public video: TikTok video links and vm.tiktok.com short links, YouTube watch / Shorts / youtu.be links, Instagram /reel/ and /p/ links, and Facebook Watch, Reels and fb.watch links.

    Why does my link show an error?

    Most errors come from a copied link that is incomplete, a private account's video, or a link from a site that isn't supported yet. Re-copy the link from the app's Share button and try again.

    Can I download from a private account?

    No. Only public videos can be fetched — private, friends-only and login-walled posts stay private. If it's your own video, switch the post to public first, or save it from inside the app.

    Which quality should I choose?

    1080p looks best on big screens, 720p is the sweet spot for phones and reposting, and 480p saves storage and data. Pick MP3 when you only want the sound. If a video was uploaded in lower quality, the highest available option is used.

    A note on copyright

    Download videos for your own offline viewing. If you repost someone else's work, credit the creator and respect their rights and each platform's terms.
